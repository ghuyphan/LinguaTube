Privacy at a Glance

  • No Ad Networks: We do not track you across the web, sell your personal data, or run third-party marketing pixels.
  • Offline-First Guest Mode: If you use Voca without signing in, all your flashcards, history, and word lists stay solely in your own browser.
  • Protected Cloud Sync: Signed-in accounts sync via Supabase PostgreSQL protected by Row Level Security (RLS) so only your account can access your data.
  • Full Data Control: You can export your vocabulary lists or request complete deletion of your account and records at any time.

1. Privacy Principles

Voca is built with an offline-first architecture. We collect only what is necessary to operate the application, schedule your vocabulary reviews, and sync your study decks between your devices.

We believe your language learning journey is personal. We do not sell your personal data or partner with data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Voca, we process the following information:

  • Account Details (when signed in): If you sign in using Google Sign-In, Supabase Auth provides us with your Google user identifier, email address, display name, and avatar image URL.
  • Study & Vocabulary Data: The vocabulary words you save, definitions, custom notes, flashcard intervals, repetitions, ease factors, watch history timestamps, and custom playlists.
  • Gamification Records: Daily practice streaks, total study XP, level progression, and completed milestones.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Browser user agent (for responsive layout handling), Cloudflare Turnstile verification tokens (to prevent bot attacks), and approximate country or region derived from Cloudflare Edge IP headers (used strictly to set appropriate default language interfaces; we never track precise GPS coordinates).
  • Guest Mode Data: If you browse and study without signing in, all study data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB and LocalStorage. None of your guest study records are transmitted to our databases.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Provide and maintain the Voca language study platform.
  • Synchronize your saved vocabulary, watch history, and decks across your phones, tablets, and computers.
  • Calculate spaced repetition (SM-2) schedules so you review words at the right time.
  • Manage Diamond credit balances for automated speech-to-text transcription.
  • Defend our servers against automated abuse, scraping floods, and denial-of-service attacks.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertising networks. We do not use your personal transcripts or saved vocabulary to train public artificial intelligence models.

4. How Data Is Stored & Protected

Client-Side Storage: Your browser stores cached dictionary results, transcript segments, and local review history in IndexedDB (lingua-tube-cache) and localStorage.

Cloud Database (Supabase PostgreSQL): Synced accounts store vocabulary, decks, and streaks in Supabase PostgreSQL hosted in secure data centers. Each table is secured with Row Level Security (RLS) policies, guaranteeing that only your authenticated Google account can read or write your personal study data.

Edge Caching (Cloudflare): Public video metadata, static dictionary lexicons, and transcript caches are cached across Cloudflare's global edge network (Cloudflare D1, KV, and R2) to provide fast loading times worldwide.

Encryption: All communication between your device and Voca is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Voca relies on the following third-party infrastructure services:

  • Google & YouTube: For Google OAuth account authentication and embedded YouTube video playback. Video streaming is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: For cloud database hosting, authentication token verification, and Row Level Security enforcement.
  • Gladia: For speech-to-text audio transcription when you request AI subtitles for a video without human captions. Audio is processed to generate subtitle cues. Gladia does not retain user audio for model training.
  • Cloudflare: For edge CDN distribution, DDoS mitigation, and Turnstile bot protection challenges.
  • Payment Processors (PayOS): For membership checkout. Payment transactions are executed directly by the PCI-compliant payment gateway; Voca does not store or process credit card numbers or banking credentials.

6. Cookies & Browser Storage

Voca uses browser local storage and essential session cookies strictly for core functionality:

  • Maintaining your active login session across page refreshes.
  • Remembering your interface language, learning language, and script display mode (furigana, pinyin, or romanization).
  • Persisting your theme preference (dark or light mode).
  • Caching study cards and dictionary lookups for offline use.

We do not use advertising cookies, social tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.

7. Your Rights & Data Control

You have full ownership and control over your personal study data:

  • Export: You can export your saved vocabulary items from the vocabulary notebook at any time.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can request full deletion of your cloud account and all synced records by contacting our support team. Upon confirmation, your records will be permanently removed from our databases.
  • Local Reset: You can clear all offline study data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or IndexedDB storage.

8. Children's Privacy

Voca is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements or legal obligations. When changes occur, the updated date at the top of this document will be revised.
