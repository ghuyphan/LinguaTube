2. What Voca Provides

Voca is an immersion language study workspace designed for learners of Japanese, Chinese, Korean, and English. The platform provides:

  • Interactive dual subtitles with pronunciation guides (furigana, pinyin, and romanization).
  • Morphological tokenization and instant dictionary definitions from open community lexicons.
  • Spaced repetition system (SM-2 SRS) flashcards for vocabulary memorization.
  • Automated speech-to-text audio transcription for videos lacking human subtitles.
  • Offline-first local storage and optional cloud synchronization via Supabase.

3. YouTube Content & Embedded Playback

By using Voca to watch YouTube content, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy.

Availability: Video playback depends on creator settings and YouTube availability. If a video is deleted, marked private, geo-blocked, or restricted by its owner on YouTube, it will immediately become unavailable on Voca.

4. Accounts & Authentication

You can use Voca as a guest without creating an account. In guest mode, your saved words, history, and study progress are stored solely in your local browser storage.

If you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is handled through Supabase Auth. We receive your basic profile (Google user ID, display name, email, and avatar) to sync your vocabulary, study streak, and playlists across your devices.

You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials. You must be at least 13 years old (or the legal age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) to register an account.

5. Your Data & Ownership

You retain full ownership of the personal study content you create, including saved words, custom definitions, study tags, notes, and playlists.

You grant Voca only the technical permissions necessary to store, back up, and synchronize this data back to your authorized sessions. You can export your vocabulary or delete your stored records at any time.

6. AI Audio Transcription & Credits

When authentic human captions are unavailable for a video, Voca offers automated speech-to-text audio transcription powered by Gladia.

Transcription consumes digital Diamond credits based on video length. Free accounts receive a monthly allotment. Extended allowances and support for longer videos are included with Supporter and Founder memberships.

Diamond credits are consumable utility tokens used exclusively to fund edge transcription processing. Credits have no cash value, cannot be redeemed for legal currency, and cannot be transferred between accounts.
